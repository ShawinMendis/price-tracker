Sale events: real discount or not?

Needs 14 days of history before it judges anything. Gimmick = price raised just before the sale so the "discount" only returns it to normal, an advertised "was" price that was never actually charged, or a claimed discount much bigger than the real one. Real deal = at least 10% below usual and at the lowest price in the last 90 days.
